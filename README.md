Performatively is a free e-reader for the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8. Read on the big
inner screen, and the outer cover screen shows your book's cover, so everyone can
see what you're reading.

## Features
- Reads EPUB, PDF and comics (CBZ, CBR, CB7)
- Page curl animation like a real book. Swipe anywhere, or tap the sides
- 1 or 2 columns on the inner screen
- Page fit for comics and PDFs: whole page, fit width (great for long strips), or fit height
- Pure black dark mode, with dark pages for PDFs
- Cover screen in color, black and white, or just the title, with brightness control
- 36 stickers you earn by reading. Put them on your book covers, and they show on the cover screen too
- Your books folder becomes your library, and each folder inside it a category

## Install
1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow your browser or file app to install unknown apps.
3. Open Performatively and choose the folder where you keep your books.

To read with the cover screen on, unfold the phone and open a book.

**Updates:** download the new APK from Releases and install it over the old one.
Your library and stickers stay. To get updates automatically, add this page's link
to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium).

## Requirements
- Android 14 or newer
- The cover screen feature is made for the Galaxy Z Fold 8. On other phones, the
  reader works but there is no cover screen.

## Privacy
Performatively has no internet access at all. Your books, reading progress and
stickers never leave your phone. There are no ads and no tracking.
